About me

I focus on high-quality 3D asset creation and environment art for games. My primary workflow includes creating clean 3D geometry, baking high-poly detail onto optimized game-ready models, texturing in Substance Painter, and setting up interactive environment scenes in Unreal Engine.

Driven by worldbuilding and game design, I constantly refine my technical pipeline for modeling, baking, texturing, and scene optimization to deliver production-ready visuals.
